What does the system do?
-At a high level, the system we built is the digital engine for our garden center's inventory. It is a secure, centralized system that keeps track of our products, our pricing, our stock levels, and how we organize everything into distinct store sections or categories. It also allows us to modifey and delete any of these.



What value does this system provide?

The value that this system provides is that it acts as a single source of truth. If a cashier at the front desk looks up the price of Japanese Maples, and the inventory manager in the back greenhouse updates the stock count for those same trees, they are both interacting with this exact system.
How could it be used?
To give you a real-world example, let's say a shipment of 50 new shovels arrives. The receiving team will use our system to immediately add those 50 shovels to our active inventory. As another example, if the wholesale cost of our fertilizer goes up, management can update the cost in our system, and it will immediately flag if our retail selling price needs to be adjusted to prevent us from losing money.

I also want to briefly mention our development process. To build this efficiently, we utilized Artificial Intelligence to act as an assistant. We used AI to help write repetitive setup code and to generate realistic test data like plant and tools products. This allowed our human engineering team to focus entirely on the complex business rules and making sure the system works perfectly.
