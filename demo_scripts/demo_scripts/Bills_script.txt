What does our system do?
- Our system manages the storage, maintenence, and validity of information
  pertaining to retail buisness operations for a garden center
- Our system ensures that product information is maintained with a standard
  that allows users access to readable and verified data. Our system ensures
  that anyone wishing to add information or change information does so correctly
  and in a matter that will not lead to confusion later on.
- Our system can be implemented into POS systems so that an array of these systems
  are constantly updated with information that represents the most current state
  of the stores inventory. It can also be implemented into an online webstore allowing
  customers to make fofillable purchases without worrying about buying the wrong product
  or putting in orders for products that are out of stock.
- AI was used to contribute to ideas and feedback on the development of our system. It allowed
  us to gain other perspectives in a quick matter which helped emmensly during the testing of
  our system.
- Our system works through a protocal of information sharing formally known as REST api. 
  This protocal performs actions based on a unit of instruction called a request.
  Simular to how a customer requests to buy a product and a retail worker respondes to their
  request, REST api's work in a simular fashon.  Our system expects to receive many requests from
  multiple different systems (for example POS systems). Our system then processes these requests
  and performs a set of actions depending on what the requests ask our system to do. We then send
  a response back to the system that sends us a request that either gives them the information
  they are requesting or confirms that our system has performed the action they have asked us to do.
